What the tags mean
You blocked

You block this author, directly or through a block list you subscribe to.

Mutual block

You and this author block each other.

Third party

The block is between two other people in the thread, usually the author and the person replying to or quoting them. The inspector checks public block records to say which way it goes.

Unavailable

Deleted, or from an account that is deactivated or taken down.
